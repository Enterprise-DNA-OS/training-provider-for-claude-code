# Training record checks

Checked 28 September 2026. Scope: selected record checks for AU fee-for-service training and NZ private training establishments. This is not approval, accreditation or a full compliance assessment. A training professional must confirm the provider's jurisdiction and obligations.

## Australia

[ASQA: Integrity of Nationally Recognised Training](https://www.asqa.gov.au/for-providers/standards-for-RTOs/practice-guides/compliance-standards-for-rtos/integrity-of-nationally-recognised-training-practice-guide) describes certification eligibility, issuance timing and retention. The check flags completed, requirements-confirmed and fees-confirmed enrolments past a thirty-day review date without a recorded credential. Staff reconcile actual fee eligibility, exemptions and withdrawal cases. The base only supports complete-course credential recording; partial-completion statements remain an external review.

AU credential references have a thirty-year retention floor measured from issuance. Assessment-material references for completed training have a two-year floor. Checks compare dates; they do not prove that files exist, are readable, or have been backed up. The base keeps all result records and does not delete anything.

[USI provider guidance](https://www.usi.gov.au/providers): record external verification or an exemption reference before recording a regulated AU credential. A ten-character format check is not verification. Demo identifiers and evidence are fictional. The free base has no USI service connection.

[NCVER software register and reporting fields](https://www.ncver.edu.au/rto-hub/avetmiss-compliant-software-register): missing date of birth is one preliminary reporting-data check. The base does not collect every AVETMISS field, validate NAT files, appear on the compliant software register or submit training activity. It is not endorsed by NCVER. Use the provider's accepted reporting process. Fee-for-service status does not remove reporting duties.

## New Zealand

[NZQA: Private Training Establishment Rules 2026](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/about-us/rules-fees-policies/nzqa-rules/pte-registration-rules/) replaced the 2022 record rules on 19 January 2026. Selected checks flag missing address, birth date or citizenship; enrolment retention below two years after completion; and academic awards not marked permanent. Academic results and credit evidence are retained without a deletion workflow. Assessment-material references have a one-year floor after completion. Attendance records are kept; their completeness requires review against the timetable.

These checks cover only part of rules 7 and 8. They do not implement student fee protection, quality assurance, international learner duties, all enrolment fields or government returns.

## Operational policies, not legal thresholds

Fourteen quiet days triggers learner follow-up. Absences and missing past-session marks trigger an attendance review. No universal attendance percentage is treated as law. Trainers' portfolio references are recorded; this base does not determine their professional eligibility. The registrar confirms full course requirements beyond the unit checklist. The fees-confirmed flag includes agreed fees outside the imported invoices.

Printed results are drafts. Do not put an official qualification logo, signature or seal on them and call them issued credentials. Record official documents only after authorised issuance elsewhere. Protect personal data, restrict database access, and back up both records and evidence. A database administrator can override application checks.
